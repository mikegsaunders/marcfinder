# marcfinder for Command Palette: Privacy Policy

marcfinder doesn't collect, store or transmit any personal information.

The extension looks up MARC 21 field definitions from reference data built into the app. It
makes no network connections, sends no analytics or usage data, and doesn't read or save your
files. Your searches stay on your device and aren't recorded.

Choosing "Open LOC documentation" opens the Library of Congress website in your browser, where
the Library of Congress's own privacy policy applies. The copy commands put text on your
clipboard only when you choose them.

PowerToys and Command Palette are Microsoft products with their own privacy terms.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/mikegsaunders/marcfinder/issues>.
